Section 1

The Role & Working Style

You are joining NearPop — a hyperlocal discovery platform — as a Field Marketing Executive. Your primary mission is to partner with local shops and merchants, introduce them to the NearPop app, and successfully list their business on our platform.

This is a flexible, performance-based contractual role. There are no mandatory shifts or fixed working hours. You have the freedom to decide your own schedule, with your earnings directly tied to your daily achievements.

Section 2

Core Responsibilities

  • 🔵Engage: Visit local shops within your assigned territory.
  • 🔵Educate & Onboard: Explain the benefits of NearPop, help merchants sign up, and complete their listing.
  • 🔵Activate: Ensure every onboarded merchant has at least 3 active listings on the app.
  • 🔵Report: Log every shop visit into the NearPop field tracker on the same day it occurs.
  • 🔵Communicate: Notify the Company in advance if you are unable to work on any given day.
Section 3

Compensation & Rewards Structure

Monthly Base Pay ₹10,000 per calendar month — paid on the 7th of the following month via UPI or bank transfer.
Mid-month joiners: Pay is calculated as — ₹10,000 ÷ Total days in month × Days worked.
Uninformed absence deduction: ₹400 per day deducted from base pay for each day absent without prior notice.
Daily Merchant Incentive — ₹50 per merchant Paid for every verified merchant onboarded with at least 3 active listings. Disbursed daily (same evening or next morning) via UPI after tracker verification.
Example: 5 merchants onboarded = ₹250 paid that day.
Weekly Performance Bonus — ₹200 Earned if you onboard 25 or more merchants in a single week (Monday to Sunday). Paid every Monday for the previous week. No partial payout for falling short.
Monthly Achievement Bonus — ₹500 Earned if you onboard 100 or more merchants in a calendar month. Paid on the 7th alongside base pay.
Note: Monthly target of 100 is independent of the weekly bonus target. Both can be earned together.

Maximum Possible Monthly Earnings (all targets achieved):

Component Calculation Amount
Monthly Base Pay Fixed ₹10,000
Daily Merchant Incentive 100 merchants × ₹50 ₹5,000
Weekly Performance Bonus 4 weeks × ₹200 ₹800
Monthly Achievement Bonus 100 merchants achieved ₹500
Total All targets met ₹16,300
Section 4

What Counts as a Verified Onboarding

A merchant onboarding is considered successful and eligible for payment only when all three conditions are met:

  • 1.The shop listing is created and marked Active on NearPop.
  • 2.The merchant profile has at least 3 live listings or deals.
  • 3.The visit is logged in the daily tracker on the same day it occurred.

All onboardings are verified by the Company before payment. Any dispute will be resolved within 48 hours.

Section 5

Absence & Communication Policy

Informed Absence Notify the Company in advance via WhatsApp before taking a day off. No deduction applies.
Uninformed Absence — ₹400 deduction per day If you take a day off without informing the Company, ₹400 will be deducted from your monthly base pay for that day.
Example: 2 uninformed absences = ₹800 deducted from monthly base pay.
3-Day Rule — Automatic Termination If you are absent for 3 consecutive days with no visit logs, no message, and no reason given, this agreement terminates automatically on the 3rd day with no notice required from either side.

Final Settlement on Auto-Termination:
(₹10,000 ÷ Total days in month) × Days actually worked before absence
+ All verified daily merchant incentives earned before absence
= Final payment. No further amount will be paid.
Section 6

Professional Conduct

  • ✅Maintain a polite, honest, and professional manner with all merchants at all times.
  • ✅Never make promises or guarantees outside official NearPop guidelines.
  • ❌Never collect money from any merchant under any circumstances.
  • ✅Keep all merchant data, contact details, and NearPop's internal materials strictly confidential.
  • ✅Return all physical assets (standees, print materials) immediately upon leaving the role.
Section 7

Ending This Agreement

  • 📤Voluntary Exit: Give 7 days notice via WhatsApp. All dues cleared within 15 days of last working day.
  • 📥Company Termination: 7 days notice given. All dues cleared within 15 days.
  • ⚡Immediate Termination (no notice, verified dues only): Falsifying tracker data • Collecting money from merchants • Sharing confidential information • Damaging NearPop's reputation.
  • 🔴Automatic Termination: 3 consecutive absent days with no communication (see Section 5).
Section 8

Legal Terms

  • ⚖️Dispute Resolution: Both parties will attempt to resolve any disagreement within 15 days. If unresolved, jurisdiction falls exclusively to the courts of Faridabad, Haryana.
  • 📜Governing Law: Indian Contract Act, 1872.
  • 🏢Employment Status: This is a contractual engagement. It does not constitute employment under the Factories Act, PF Act, or ESI Act.
