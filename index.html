Merchant Pitch Script

What to say when you walk into a shop. 2 minute pitch.

Read before each visit
Hindi
"Namaste ji, main NearPop se hoon. Aapki shop free mein NearPop app par list karni hai — jaise Justdial, lekin bilkul free aur seedha aapke area ke customers tak. Aapko kuch karna nahi — main abhi set kar deta hoon. Bas aapki Gmail ID de dijiye."
English
"Hi, I'm from NearPop. We're listing local shops for free on our app — customers nearby can discover you directly. No cost, no tech knowledge needed. I can set it up right now, just need your Gmail."
💬

Objection Handling

Common pushbacks and what to say

Read & remember
If they say: "Main pehle se JustDial / Google par hoon"
"Bilkul ji, woh bhi rakho. NearPop alag hai — ye sirf aapke 2-3 km ke customers ko dikhata hai, aur GPS se automatically near them pop karta hai jab woh app open karte hain. Aur ye free hai."
If they say: "Abhi busy hoon / baad mein aana"
"Theek hai ji, main bas 2 minute mein set kar deta hoon — aapko kuch karna nahi. Ya aap apna number de do, main WhatsApp par flyer bhej deta hoon, jab time ho dekh lena."
If they say: "Kya faida hoga?"
"Ye dekhiye — (show Listing Sample) — yeh aapki shop ki page hogi. Jo bhi NearPop kholega aapke area mein, unhe aap dikhenge. Ye free hai, koi subscription nahi."
