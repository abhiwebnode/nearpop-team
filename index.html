Merchant-First Execution Plan
📌 Strategy: Fill one zone completely before expanding Do not spread across all of Delhi NCR. Pick one dense market in Faridabad. Make it look alive. Then expand zone by zone.
1️⃣

Week 1–2 — Pick your zone

Choose one market (Sector 16, Old Faridabad market, or similar). Target: 50 shops in that zone only.

2️⃣

Week 3–4 — Fill the zone

Visit every shop. Pitch, onboard, place standee. Aim: zone looks full when a customer opens the app.

3️⃣

Month 2 — Push users into that zone

WhatsApp groups, society posts, customer flyers in that specific area. Show real deals from real shops.

4️⃣

Month 3+ — Expand zone by zone

Repeat the same playbook in the next market. Merchant referrals ("your neighbour is already on it") help.

Merchant Onboarding Checklist — Do this for every shop
Micro-Area Route Planning — How to plan your day
🗺️ Walk one street fully before moving to the next Do not skip shops. A skipped shop = a competitor gap. Cover every shop on a lane, then move to the next.
1
Morning (9–11 AM) — Identify the laneWalk the lane once without pitching. Count shops, note which are open. Identify the biggest / most crowded ones.
2
10–1 PM — Priority shops firstAnchor shops (chemist, grocery, restaurant) attract foot traffic. Onboard these first — others follow when they see their neighbour on the app.
3
1–2 PM — Rest / follow-up callsCall yesterday's pending merchants. Send WhatsApp templates to shops that said "kal aana".
4
2–5 PM — Remaining shops on laneFinish the lane. Do not leave any shop uncovered. Mark each on the tracker.
5
5–6 PM — Daily reportFill in the tracker. Send the summary to the founder. Plan tomorrow's lane.

Counter QR Standee / Sticker — Spec & Placement
📐 Standee Spec Size: A5 (148×210mm) for counter standee. Sticker: 10×10 cm. Material: Glossy laminated for standee; vinyl for sticker. Content: Shop QR + "Scan to see our deals on NearPop" in Hindi + English.
📍 Where to place it Counter standee → billing counter, facing the customer.
Sticker → glass door or front window at eye level.
Never put both at the same shop — pick one.
📋

QR Installation Guide

Read this before placing the standee at a shop

Step by step
1
Confirm onboarding is done firstDo not place the standee until the listing is live. The QR must link to a real page.
2
Scan to verify the QR worksBefore handing over, scan it yourself. Make sure it opens the correct shop page in NearPop.
3
Place at billing counter — merchant's rightCustomer-facing side. At arm's reach. Not behind the counter where customers can't see.
4
Explain to the merchantSay: "Jab bhi koi customer aaye, aap bata sakte ho — 'Hamare deals NearPop par hain, scan karo.' "
5
Take a photo of the placed standeePhoto proof for your daily report. Also useful for showing other merchants what it looks like.

Merchant Pitch Script

What to say when you walk into a shop. 2 minute pitch.

English

Objection Handling

Common pushbacks and what to say

Read & remember
If they say: "Main pehle se JustDial / Google par hoon"
"Bilkul ji, woh bhi rakho. NearPop alag hai — ye sirf aapke 2-3 km ke customers ko dikhata hai, aur GPS se automatically near them pop karta hai jab woh app open karte hain. Aur ye free hai."
If they say: "Abhi busy hoon / baad mein aana"
"Theek hai ji, main bas 2 minute mein set kar deta hoon — aapko kuch karna nahi. Ya aap apna WhatsApp number de do, main flyer bhej deta hoon, jab time ho dekh lena."
If they say: "Kya faida hoga?"
"Ye dekhiye — (show Listing Sample) — yeh aapki shop ki page hogi. Jo bhi NearPop kholega aapke area mein, unhe aap dikhenge. Ye free hai, koi subscription nahi."

Data Collection & Privacy Rules — Read before collecting any data
📋 What field staff may collect Shop name, address, category, and owner's Gmail (for listing ownership). Phone number is optional and only for follow-up — never mandatory.
  • ✅ Collect Gmail only if the merchant gives it voluntarily. Never pressure.
  • ✅ Phone numbers are for your follow-up only — do not share with anyone else.
  • ✅ Shop photos: take only exterior / product photos. Never take photos of the owner without consent.
  • ✅ Log data in the tracker sheet only — not in personal WhatsApp chats or notes.
  • ❌ Do not promise anything beyond the free listing — no guarantees of customers, no revenue promises.
  • ❌ Do not share merchant contact details with anyone outside the NearPop team.
  • ❌ Do not create listings for shops that explicitly said no. Respect opt-outs.
  • ❌ Do not collect any government IDs. Not needed, not allowed.
